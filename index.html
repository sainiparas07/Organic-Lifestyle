Crop Calendar

A simple seasonal planning guide. Actual sowing dates should be adjusted according to local climate, soil and crop variety.

Crop Season Sowing Period Harvest Period Basic Note
Wheat Rabi Oct–Dec Mar–Apr Needs suitable soil moisture and cool weather.
Mustard Rabi Oct–Nov Feb–Mar Common winter oilseed crop.
Gram Rabi Oct–Nov Feb–Mar Suitable for comparatively dry conditions.
Rice Kharif Jun–Jul Sep–Nov Requires adequate water availability.
Maize Kharif Jun–Jul Sep–Oct Plan according to rainfall and local variety.
Cotton Kharif Apr–Jun Oct–Jan Warm-season crop with a longer growing period.
Tomato Multiple Varies by region Varies by variety Use a locally suitable variety and planting window.
Potato Rabi Oct–Nov Jan–Mar Cool-season vegetable crop.
