🌿 Organic Fertilizers & Plant Nutrition

Organic fertilizers improve soil fertility naturally, increase crop yield, protect the environment, and produce healthy chemical-free food.

Vermicompost

  • Uses: Vegetables, Fruits, Flowers
  • Nutrients: Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium
  • Apply: Before sowing or planting
  • Time: Every 45–60 days
  • Efficiency: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  • Price: ₹10–15/kg

Cow Dung Manure

  • Uses: All Crops
  • Nutrients: Organic Carbon
  • Apply: Mix with soil
  • Time: Every 2–3 months
  • Efficiency: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆
  • Price: ₹3–6/kg

Neem Cake

  • Uses: Pest & Disease Control
  • Nutrients: Nitrogen Rich
  • Apply: Around root zone
  • Time: Every Month
  • Efficiency: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  • Price: ₹35–50/kg

Compost

  • Uses: Garden & Field Crops
  • Nutrients: Organic Matter
  • Apply: Mix before planting
  • Time: Every 60 days
  • Efficiency: ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆
  • Price: ₹8–12/kg

Mustard Cake

  • Uses: Vegetables & Fruit Plants
  • Nutrients: Nitrogen
  • Apply: Liquid Fertilizer
  • Time: Every 20 Days
  • Efficiency: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  • Price: ₹40–50/kg

🌱 How to Use Organic Fertilizers

1️⃣ Prepare the Soil
2️⃣ Mix Organic Manure
3️⃣ Wait 7–10 Days
4️⃣ Plant Seeds
5️⃣ Apply Liquid Fertilizer Every 15–20 Days
6️⃣ Harvest Healthy Crops 🌾
